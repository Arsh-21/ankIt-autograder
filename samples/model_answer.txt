Q1. During backpropagation the gradient at an early layer is a product of many local derivatives. With sigmoid or tanh these derivatives are often much smaller than 1, so the product shrinks exponentially with depth and the early layers barely update. Using ReLU (derivative 1 for positive inputs) or residual connections (which give the gradient a direct path backwards) reduces the problem.

import math
def softmax(x):
    m = max(x)
    exps = [math.exp(v - m) for v in x]
    s = sum(exps)
    return [e / s for e in exps]
