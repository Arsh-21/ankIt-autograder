Q7. def softmax(x):
        m = max(x)
        e = [math.exp(v - m) for v in x]
        s = sum(e)
        return [v / s for v in e]
    e^(x-m) / sum e^(x-m) = e^x / sum e^x since e^-m
    cancels; biggest exponent is 0 so no overflow.
